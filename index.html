FAQ

Everything you need to know before we start.

How long does a website project take?
Most landing pages take 3-7 days. Small business sites usually take 2-3 weeks depending on content readiness and revision rounds.
Do I own the site after it's built?
Yes — 100% ownership. You get full access to all code, assets, and accounts. No lock-in, no ongoing fees unless you choose maintenance.
Can you improve my existing site's SEO?
Absolutely. I can help with on-page structure, technical basics, metadata, performance optimization, and other SEO improvements.
What if I don't have a logo or brand yet?
That's fine. I can work with what you have and help guide you toward a cohesive visual identity, or connect you with brand designers I trust.
Do you offer hosting or maintenance?
Yes. I can help set up hosting and offer ongoing maintenance packages to keep your site updated, secure, and performing well.
